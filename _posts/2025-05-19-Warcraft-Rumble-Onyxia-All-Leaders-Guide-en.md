## Warcraft Rumble Onyxia Guide - including All Leaders Strategy and Low Avg Level Strategy

After clearing Onyxia with every leader, I started a brand new account to challenge it again with an even lower-level approach.

For higher-average-level strategies, please refer to the old article below. The lower-average-level strategies are now updated at the top of this article.

Basically, the core clearing principles are:

1. Have at least two different minis that can clear Whelp Eggs.
2. AoE damage to deal with Dragons and big clumps of units.
3. Enough damage to deal with Earth Elementals — otherwise, you'll have to rely on Skeleton Party.
4. A tank that can hold against the Dragon Guard, or a deck that can quickly remove the Dragon Guard.

## 【Common Deck Composition】

Aside from Old Murk-Eye's unique deck, most other decks follow a fairly consistent pattern.
In my experience, you need at least two minis that can clear whelps on their own, then fine-tune the deck based on the leader.

- **Tank**: Fire Elemental or other tank leader. (Fire Elemental's Immolation Aura is incredibly useful)
- **AoE Damage**: Pyromancer or Huntress. (Pyromancer is more versatile; Huntress costs more, but pairs well with Malfurion)
- **Healer**: Shaman or Priestess. (Use Shaman if you need armor, Priestess if gold is tight)
- **Home Defense**: Skeleton Party (few setups don't need it).
- **Whelp Clear**: Witch Doctor or Gryphon Rider or Firehammer or Drake or Troll. (For low levels, I recently like Witch Doctor + Vulture)
- **Other**: Bandit or Faerie Dragon or spells or other minis you find useful (use Bandit if low on gold, Faerie Dragon for resistance)

## 【Phased Strategy】

Although each leader's strategy differs, Onyxia's phases (P1, P2, P3) share some common challenges:

- **P1**: You need a reliable way to deal with Whelp Eggs. Besides the whelp clear minis mentioned above, if your Witch Doctor isn't high enough level, pair it with Vulture — don't rely on just one mini.
- **P2**: Prioritize holding the front towers and dealing with Earth Elementals reliably. Skeleton Party is an easy counter.
- **P3**: High DPS comps can push right side to rush the boss during overtime. Lower DPS comps should take out the Dragon Guard, reclaim the front towers, then attack from both sides.
